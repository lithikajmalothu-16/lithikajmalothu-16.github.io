Panther Air

Classroom energy and air-quality pilot, Fall 2026

Classroom heating, cooling, and lighting are supposed to follow how rooms are used. Often they don't, and nobody checks. I led a proposal to GSU's Campus Sustainability Grant that pairs occupancy, CO2, and energy readings to flag rooms that are under-ventilated or conditioned while empty. I own the measurement plan, timeline, and budget, and I'm recruiting a faculty advisor to take it into deployment.

Battery storage optimizer

Python, LightGBM, PuLP, U.S. EIA data

A battery earns money by charging when power is cheap and selling when it's expensive, so the plan is only as good as the price forecast. I built a day-ahead forecast on EIA wholesale data and a dispatch model on top of it, then stress-tested the result. Leaving out battery wear made returns look about three times better than reality, enough to flip the investment call.

58%lower forecast error than a naive baseline
66.5%of perfect-foresight profit over a 72-day backtest
